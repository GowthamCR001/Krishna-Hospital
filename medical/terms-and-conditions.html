Telemedicine & Online Consultation Terms

4.1 Legal Framework

All telemedicine and teleconsultation services provided via the website operate in strict accordance with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020 issued by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW), Government of India.

4.2 Patient & Practitioner Requirements
  • Mutual Identification: Both the Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) and the patient must verify their identities at the commencement of the consultation.
  • Explicit Consent: Patient consent is mandatory before starting any teleconsultation session.
  • Truthful Disclosure: Patients are required to disclose their full medical history, pre-existing conditions, allergies, and current medications accurately.
4.3 Limitations of Telemedicine
  • Clinical Discretion: An RMP reserves the absolute right to discontinue a teleconsultation at any point if they determine, in their professional judgment, that an in-person clinical examination or emergency intervention is required.
  • Prescription Restrictions: Medicines listed under Schedule X of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1945, and any controlled substances governed by the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985, cannot be prescribed via telemedicine.
