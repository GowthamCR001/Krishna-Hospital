24 × 7 Emergency & Trauma Care

Immediate trauma, casualty response & acute resuscitation in Hassan

The Emergency & Trauma Department at Krishna Hospital operates round-the-clock, 365 days a year, providing immediate clinical care for medical emergencies, severe accidents, acute cardiac events, and critical injuries. Staffed by qualified emergency medical officers, trauma surgeons, and trained nursing staff, our casualty unit is equipped with multi-parameter monitors, resuscitation bays, and emergency surgical backup to save lives in the critical golden hour.

Department Strengths & Facilities

  • Rapid triage and immediate casualty resuscitation bay
  • Advanced trauma care protocol for road traffic accidents and head injuries
  • Immediate access to multi-slice CT scanning and emergency digital X-ray
  • Direct integration with 24-Hour Cardiac Ambulance and Adult/Paediatric ICUs
  • On-site emergency minor OT for lacerations, fractures, and acute wounds
  • 24/7 in-house pharmacy and diagnostic lab support for rapid reports

Key Treatments & Clinical Services

Accident & Polytrauma Care

Emergency stabilization, wound management, fracture splinting, and immediate surgical triage.

Cardiac Emergencies

Rapid ECG, initial clot-busting therapy, oxygen stabilization, and intensive cardiac monitoring.

Acute Stroke Protocol

Immediate CT brain imaging and neuro-resuscitation within the critical therapeutic window.

Poisoning & Toxicology

Gastric lavage, targeted antidotes, vital organ support, and continuous ICU surveillance.

Burns & Wound Management

Sterile wound debridement, fluid resuscitation, and immediate plastic surgery support.

Respiratory Distress

Invasive and non-invasive emergency ventilation, oxygen delivery, and nebulization care.
